Our School

📖 History

Mandaue City Science National High School, founded in 1996 as Mandaue City National Science and Technology High School, offers a special curriculum with intensive programs in Science, Mathematics, English, and electives like SPFL French and Research. Originally operating in Opao, Mandaue City, it moved to Ibabao-Estancia in 2008.

The school was established to provide advanced education for students excelling in Science and Mathematics, beginning with an initial intake of 25 students. Students must maintain an 87% overall weighted average, with minimum grades of 87% in Math, English, and Science, and 85% in other subjects to remain enrolled. The school gained independence in 1999 and has since earned recognition through student achievements, with support from city officials including Mayor Thadeo Ouano and Barangay Chairman Joy Mangubat.
