🎶 Cultural & Performing Arts

Cultural Performances

Mandaue City Science High School has participated in cultural and performing arts events, showcasing Filipino culture through dance and other performances.

The school's Dance Company has performed in both local competitions and international cultural exchange events.

🇵🇭 2017 Cebu City Korea Day

In 2017, the Mandaue City Science High School Dance Company opened the evening at the 7th Cebu City Korea Day, an international cultural exchange event.

The students performed traditional Filipino dances while wearing traditional Filipino clothing.

The Dance Company shared the program with Korean performing groups from Seoul and Busan, allowing the students to represent Filipino culture alongside international performers.

🌟 2025 GSK Ceremony

In 2025, the ManSci Dance Company opened the GSK (Gawad sa Kabataan) ceremony.

The event was organized by RAFI (Ramon Aboitiz Foundation Inc.), one of Cebu's major foundations focused on youth development and cultural heritage.

RAFI's President and CEO, Amaya Aboitiz, spoke during the event about the importance of young people carrying forward Cebuano cultural heritage.

Opening Performance

The ManSci Dance Company was selected to open the program, highlighting the school's role in promoting cultural heritage through performing arts.

🏆 Regional Festival of Talents 2025

At RFOT 2025 (Regional Festival of Talents) in the Division of Talisay City, Mandaue City Science High School achieved strong results in performing arts.

🥈 Dance Sports

Mandaue City Science High School placed 1st Runner-Up in Dance Sports.

🏆 Speech Choir

In the same festival, the school also won Champion in Speech Choir.

💃 2014 Folk Dance Competition

The school's achievements in cultural performances extend further back.

In 2014, Mandaue City Science High School placed 2nd in a folk dance competition.

The school performed "Lanceros de Tayabas", a traditional Filipino folk dance.

This demonstrates that the school's involvement in competitive cultural performances has continued for more than a decade.

🌏 Promoting Filipino Culture

Through its Dance Company and performing arts activities, Mandaue City Science High School provides students with opportunities to represent Filipino and Cebuano culture.

From local competitions to international cultural exchange events, these performances demonstrate the school's continuing involvement in cultural and performing arts.
