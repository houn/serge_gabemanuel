🔬 International Science & Engineering

🌎 Regeneron International Science and Engineering Fair

In May 2024, two Grade 12 students from Mandaue City Science National High School represented the Philippines at the Regeneron International Science and Engineering Fair (ISEF) 2024.

May 11–17, 2024
Los Angeles, California

Regeneron ISEF is a major international science and engineering competition for high school students. The 2024 fair brought together young researchers from around the world.

🇵🇭 Representing the Philippines

The school was represented by two Grade 12 students who qualified after competing in the 2024 National Science and Technology Fair (NSTF).

Both students placed among the Top 8 nationally at the National Science and Technology Fair, earning the opportunity to represent the Philippines at ISEF.

🚁 Karl Gian S. Abellana

Enhanced Crop Disease Symptom Detection in Corn Using Drone Aerial Imagery and AI

Karl's research focused on detecting disease symptoms in corn (Zea mays) using drone aerial imagery and artificial intelligence.

The project explored how aerial images and AI could be used to identify and map symptoms of crop diseases.

🌾 Christian Paul B. Palacio

Improving Biodiesel Production Using Rice Bran

Christian's research investigated the use of rice bran, also known as "darak", as a source for improving biodiesel production.

The project explored how an agricultural byproduct could be transformed into a usable energy source.

🧪 A Year of Research

Both students spent approximately one year developing their research projects before reaching the international competition.

Their participation demonstrates the school's commitment to developing scientific research, innovation, and problem-solving among its students.

🏫 School & Community Support

The students' achievement was supported by their teachers, coaches, and the school's science program.

Science coordinator Erlinda Parba highlighted the significance of the students' achievement and their participation on the international stage.

The students and their coaches also paid a courtesy visit to Mandaue City Hall following their achievement. Their accomplishment was also featured by GMA Regional TV.

🇵🇭 Philippine Delegation

The two Mandaue City Science National High School students were part of a wider Philippine delegation at the international fair.

Other participating Philippine students came from schools including Quezon City Science High School, Angeles City Science High School, City of Bogo Senior High School, and Cebu City National Science High School.

The participation of students from different Philippine science schools demonstrated the country's representation in an international science and engineering competition.

🏆 Why This Achievement Matters

Representing the Philippines at ISEF was a significant achievement for Mandaue City Science National High School.

It highlighted the students' dedication to research and demonstrated the school's continued development of young scientists capable of presenting their work on an international stage.
