📝 Campus Journalism

Campus Journalism at Mandaue City Science National High School

Mandaue City Science National High School has a strong history in campus journalism, with students consistently participating and earning recognition in division, regional, and national competitions.

The school's journalism program covers different areas of campus journalism, including individual and group competitions, broadcasting, online publishing, and collaborative desktop publishing in both English and Filipino.

🏆 2025–2026 Division Schools Press Conference

At the 2025 Division Schools Press Conference (DSPC), held on January 27, 2026 at Basak Elementary School, Mandaue City Science National High School was named the overall top-performing school at the secondary level.

The school was recognized as the overall top-performing secondary school among roughly 600 campus journalists, advisers, and coaches across the Mandaue division.

📰 Journalism Competition Achievements

The school's journalists achieved strong results across a wide range of categories.

Individual and Group Categories

Students earned recognition in individual and group journalism competitions.

Broadcasting

The school competed successfully in broadcasting categories as part of its campus journalism program.

Online Publishing

Students also participated in online publishing categories, demonstrating their skills in digital journalism.

Collaborative Desktop Publishing

The school also earned recognition in collaborative desktop publishing competitions.

✍️ Individual Recognition

Jashaira Lovei Heyrosa

Jashaira Lovei Heyrosa placed 1st in Filipino sports writing during the 2025 Division Schools Press Conference.

She credited her coach's guidance and her own self-belief for the achievement.

🌎 Advancement to the Regional Level

The school's success at the division level qualified its delegation to advance to the 2026 Regional Schools Press Conference (RSPC).

The regional competition was scheduled for March 2, 2026 in Danao City.

🇵🇭 National Schools Press Conference

The school's journalism program has also reached the national level.

A Manila Bulletin article published in July 2026 featured Grade 12 student and sports writer Karla Charismae C. Dela Rama, describing her experience at her first National Schools Press Conference (NSPC).

The National Schools Press Conference is the highest level of campus journalism competition in the Philippines.

📚 The Campus Journalism Competition Path

Journalism competitions in the Philippines follow a progression from the division level to the national level.

Division

Division Schools Press Conference (DSPC)

Regional

Regional Schools Press Conference (RSPC)

National

National Schools Press Conference (NSPC)

⭐ A Strong Journalism Tradition

Mandaue City Science High School has been described as one of the division's traditional "perennial winners" in secondary-level campus journalism competitions.

This reflects a sustained track record of achievement rather than a single recent success.
